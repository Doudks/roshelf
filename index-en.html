How to install RoShelf

The current beta requires manual installation. Follow these steps using Google Chrome or Brave.

1
Download RoShelf

Click "Download RoShelf" above. On the GitHub release page, find the "Assets" section and download the v0.10 ZIP file.

2
Extract the ZIP file

Extract the ZIP into a permanent folder. Do not delete or move this folder after installing the extension.

3
Open the extensions page

In Chrome, open chrome://extensions. In Brave, open brave://extensions.

4
Enable Developer Mode

Turn on the "Developer mode" switch on the extensions page.

5
Load the extension

Click "Load unpacked" and select the extracted folder containing the manifest.json file.

6
Open Roblox

Visit roblox.com. Open the Roblox sidebar and click "RoShelf".

7
Connect your account

Click the connect button and authorize RoShelf through the official Roblox login flow. You can then start organizing games into your lists.

Notice: Beta version

The RoShelf OAuth application is awaiting Roblox approval. Until it is approved, new users may be unable to complete authorization. The extension is currently available for testing.

Privacy and security

RoShelf uses Roblox OAuth 2.0 to identify users and protect game list editing.

The application may store your Roblox user ID, username, display name, avatar, and categorized games using Supabase. Lists are public to users who have the extension installed.

RoShelf never asks for your Roblox password. Authorization takes place through Roblox's official system.
