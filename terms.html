By installing or using RoShelf, you agree to these terms and the Privacy Policy. You must also follow Roblox's terms and the OAuth requirements applicable to your account. Do not use the extension if you disagree.

2. Service

RoShelf lets users create custom categories, organize games, set private categories, feature favorites, compare ratings, and import publicly listed games from other profiles. It is not an official Roblox service, does not operate Roblox games, and does not provide in-game advantages.

3. Identity and authorization

Editing a list requires authorizing account identification through Roblox OAuth. The server links changes to the authenticated account's user ID. Viewing public lists does not require signing in. Never provide your Roblox password or session cookie to RoShelf or anyone else.

4. Public content and conduct

Public categories and their ratings are visible to other extension users. Categories marked private do not appear in public queries.
